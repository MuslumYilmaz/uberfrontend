# Shopee: reported frontend interview questions and interview process

> Working record started on 2026-10-21. It lists the frontend interview questions and tasks that public interview reports attribute to Shopee (Sea Group), with a source and access status for every item, and the interview stages those reports and Shopee's own pages describe. Companion documents: `docs/references/jpmorgan-agoda-xai-questions-2026-10.md` and `docs/references/jpmorgan-agoda-xai-interview-processes-2026-10.md` (the same format for the previous three companies), `docs/references/company-pages-plan-2026-10.md` (how a company page is built). ## Why Shopee (2026-10-21)

Shopee came out of the China-market check the same day: the Chinese companies with Chinese-language demand on Baidu are unreachable for an English site, and Shopee is the one company in that cultural orbit with English-language demand on Google and no editorial competitor.

- **Demand.** Google autocomplete on 2026-10-21: `shopee frontend interview`, `shopee frontend interview questions`, `shopee frontend engineer interview`, `shopee frontend engineer interview questions`, plus `shopee interview process` and `shopee interview process singapore` (the 2026-10-19 six-template sweep counted 4). Bing's suggestions are generic (`shopee interview`, `shopee case study technical interview`). Baidu autocomplete has `shopee前端面试` and `shopee公司的面试流程`, Chinese-language demand this site does not target. Trends was not measured (the Chrome Trends page would not render on 2026-10-21).
- **Our existing audience overlaps Shopee's hiring countries.** Search Console, 2026-01-03 to 2026-10-19: Vietnam 3,009 impressions and 17 clicks (the fifth-largest source of clicks), Philippines 2,637 / 2, Indonesia 1,689 / 5, Thailand 991 / 3, Malaysia 968 / 2, Singapore 514 / 3, Taiwan 206 / 1. About 10,000 impressions come from the markets Shopee hires in.
- **Competition.** The 2026-10-19 sweep of 15 prep sites (GreatFrontEnd, Front End Interview Handbook, FrontendLead, BigFrontEnd.dev, devtools.tech, LearnersBucket, frontendgeek, PracHub, techinterview.org, Aced, IGotAnOffer, Hello Interview, interviewing.io, Prepfully, Dataford) found no frontend-specific Shopee page. On 2026-10-21 the results for `shopee frontend interview questions` were Glassdoor pages, NodeFlair's general software-engineer article, the Dataford template and question list, and 1point3acres; for `shopee front end engineer interview experience singapore`, Glassdoor only. No editorial guide.

## Research rules

The rules are the ones used for the JPMorgan, Agoda and xAI round:

- A question counts only if it is on a page our tools read in full (F) or appears verbatim in a search snippet (S). Unreadable sources are marked U.
- Glassdoor, 1point3acres and Nowcoder are snippet-only. Reddit was not attempted.
- Templated pages (Dataford, Prepfully, interviewpal, NodeFlair's aggregate guide and similar) are listed as what ranks, never as evidence.
- Taro re-hosts Glassdoor reviews with a changed city and date; a report is counted once and Taro dates on the 1st are marked `*`.
- Chinese and Vietnamese first-person accounts (Juejin, Viblo, Medium) count when read in full; quotes keep the original language with an English gloss.
- Exclusions: seller, operations, customer-service and marketing roles; Shopify (a different company).

## Method

One research agent worked on 2026-10-21 under the shared brief (evidence rules, access recipes, output format). The coordinating session then re-read a sample of quotes on their pages (see "Verification").

## Verdict

- Depth: moderate and uneven. Shopee has far more public frontend material than JPMorgan, Agoda or xAI, but almost all of it is Chinese-language campus 面经 from 2019–2022 and short Glassdoor snippets. Seven first-person frontend loops were read in full with concrete questions (SH1 Vietnam 2025, SH3 China 2020, SH4 China 2022, SH5 Taipei 2021, SH6 Singapore-campus 2020, SH7 and SH8 China campus 2020), four more are first-person but thin (SH9, SH10, SH18, SH19), three strong Chinese reports are snippet-only because Zhihu and 1point3acres block reads (SH12, SH14, SH16), and about fourteen Glassdoor frontend reviews are snippet-only (SHG1–SHG12). Another dozen generic backend/platform loops (SH30–SH44) fix the stage shapes per region. No prep site has a frontend-specific Shopee guide beyond Dataford's template and NodeFlair's generic SWE guide.
- Where the reports come from: Singapore (campus hires interviewed from Taiwan, Malaysia, Vietnam and India; experienced hires from India and Vietnam), Ho Chi Minh City (Vietnamese candidates, one relocating from Hanoi), Taipei (local hires), mainland China (Shenzhen/Shanghai campus and 社招, all in Chinese), Jakarta (two Glassdoor snippets) and one Dhaka loop. Nothing frontend-specific from Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, Manila or São Paulo. Levels: fresh graduate / campus dominates (SH6–SH10, SH14, SH15, SH36, SH37, SH39); experienced 1.5–5 years (SH1, SH3, SH4, SH5, SH12, SH16, SHG2); one Senior Frontend offer (SH21) and one Taipei 資深軟體工程師 (SH23).

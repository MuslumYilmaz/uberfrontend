# Capital One: reported frontend interview questions and interview process

> Working record started on 2026-10-23. It lists the frontend interview questions and tasks that public interview reports attribute to Capital One, with a source and access status for every item, and the interview stages those reports and Capital One's own pages describe. Companion documents: `docs/references/shopee-questions-and-process-2026-10.md` (the same format), `docs/references/company-pages-plan-2026-10.md` (how a company page is built) and `reports/company-candidates-research-2026-10-23.md` (why Capital One was chosen). The user approved Capital One for the next company wave on 2026-10-23; the build is recorded under Implementation.

## Why Capital One (2026-10-23)

Chosen in the candidate round recorded in `company-pages-plan-2026-10.md` (Candidate selection rule) and approved by the user on 2026-10-23 together with Anduril and GitLab.

- **Not covered.** No GreatFrontEnd company guide and no Front End Interview Handbook company page (lists re-read on 2026-10-22).
- **Demand.** Google suggestions on 2026-10-22: `capital one front end developer interview questions`, `capital one frontend engineer interview`, `capital one frontend interview` and more; Bing returned twelve, the most of the finalists.
- **Hiring on the day.** Seven frontend-titled postings on Workday (Front-end Engineer 4 and 5, McLean, New York, Richmond and remote), the most of any finalist after Anduril. Capital One-badged users on Blind reported a hiring freeze in April 2026.
- **Readable sources.** Fifteen frontend-titled Taro accounts, nine with Power Day detail, plus Blind threads with Capital One-badged commenters. No independent first-person frontend loop exists.
- **Buyers.** United States, plus one Canadian account.

## Research rules

- A question counts only if it is on a page our tools read in full (F) or appears verbatim in a search snippet (S). Unreadable sources are marked U. Every source cited below is F.
- Search summaries were used only to find pages. Every quote below comes from the page, not the summary. Example: a search summary said a candidate wrote that "it seems like you just need to get 50%"; the page says "It seems like you only need to get 50% to pass." (CO6), and only the page's words are used.
- Not cited: Glassdoor, 1point3acres, Prepfully, Dataford, PracHub question lists, interviewpal and other templated "N questions" pages. Glassdoor review pages appear under "What ranks" only.
- Taro (jointaro.com) interview pages are Glassdoor reviews re-hosted with rewritten text. Only the public pages were read (curl). No Taro API was called. Each report is counted once. A Taro date on the 1st of a month is a placeholder: it is marked `*` and only the month is given. Taro may change the city. When Taro's outcome label contradicts the text, the text wins and the conflict is noted.
- Blind: every comment was read, including replies the page hides behind links such as "View 3 more replies...". Their text, author, badge and date are in the page's own embedded data, which was parsed from the saved HTML (no Blind API was called). Each claim is marked as the original poster's own account, a commenter's own account (c), or a plan written before the interview (p). Company badges are taken from that data, not from the rendered layout.
- LeetCode Discuss was read through the GraphQL endpoint (`ugcArticleDiscussionArticle`, with the `topic` query as a fallback). Medium was read through the author's RSS feed. dev.to was read through its public article API.
- First-person posts that end in a sales pitch are treated as templates, not evidence (CO-T1, CO-T2).
- Scope: web frontend roles in the United States and Canada, 2020 onward. Generic software-engineer Power Day accounts (CO30 to CO64) are kept apart. Front-end and full-stack candidates sit the same case and behavioral rounds, so these accounts fix stage shapes, not frontend content. Excluded: India campus loops (a different process), data, product, analyst and strategy roles, and pre-2020 frontend reports (listed under Open items).

## Method

One research session on 2026-10-23, under the brief's evidence rules.

1. Read the 15 frontend-titled Taro accounts named in the brief, in full, from their public pages. Raw copies of every page read were kept for the session (113 files).
2. Read the three named Blind threads. Then scanned Blind's Capital One search pages for "capital one front end", "capital one frontend", "capital one power day", "capital one react" and "capital one angular" (two pages each) and opened 45 threads. Forty engineering threads were read with all their comments and badges, hidden replies included; five product and strategy threads were skipped.
3. Ran 9 WebSearch calls on jointaro.com, teamblind.com, leetcode.com, medium.com and dev.to (the limit was 10), one general WebSearch to record what ranks, and 3 searches on capitalonecareers.com and capitalone.com. Scanned the careers sitemap for interview pages.
4. Read 11 LeetCode posts through GraphQL, 10 more Taro pages (full-stack and generic loops), one Medium post through RSS and two dev.to posts through the article API.
5. Read 14 careers-site URLs (8 with interview content, one 404). Queried Capital One's Workday job search API for frontend titles and saved all 7 frontend-titled postings. Four were read in full; all 7 were checked by script for the stack line, the years required and the BFF and agentic AI lines (six of seven mention BFFs; the SEO role does not).
6. Mapped reported tasks to existing catalog items in `cdn/questions` (read-only), checked against the hash-pinned ids in `backend/content/interview/authoring/interview-coding-registry-v1.authoring.json` and the pinned system design bundles.
7. Checked every quotation in this file by script against the saved page text (see Verification).

## Verdict

- **Depth: moderate, and older than it looks.** There are fifteen frontend-titled Taro accounts from 2020 to 2025. Nine describe Power Day rounds or questions (CO1 to CO9). Six are thin or old: an assessment only (CO10), a recruiter call only (CO15), and four short 2020 to 2021 accounts from before or at the start of the current loop (CO11 to CO14). The newest frontend account with round detail is CO4 (2024-11*, "design a credit card account"). The newest with a full loop is CO1 (2024-10*, build a single-page application). The newest frontend-titled account of any kind is CO10 (2025-05*): an online assessment and a rejection. No first-person frontend loop exists on Blind, LeetCode, Medium or dev.to. Blind has five frontend plans (CO21 to CO25) whose comments, some from Capital One-badged users, describe the assessment and the generic loop. One 2025 Taro account (CO16) comes from a "front-end-focused engineer" who sat the generic full-stack Power Day.
- **What recurs in frontend loops, 2022 to 2024.** A CodeSignal assessment of about 70 minutes with four LeetCode-style questions, where partial completion can pass (CO1, CO4, CO5, CO6, CO8; CO2 says three questions). Then a Power Day of four one-hour interviews on Zoom, back to back:
  1. Frontend design: "design a credit card information and application page, followed by a confirmation page" (CO2), "FE system design of the credit card application page" (CO3), "design a credit card account" (CO4), or design a web application or SPA from requirements (CO5, CO6).
  2. Frontend technical: build a single-page application as pair programming (CO1), a React project judged on components and coding style (CO2), an Angular form with validations (CO5), an Angular form that adds messages to a list (CO6), or trivia and concepts with no coding (CO8).
  3. A case round built on code reading: a bug in an intern's budgeting program in Java, C++, Node.js or Python, found without writing the fix (CO2); a cost-calculation business case that ends in a faulty algorithm to fix (CO6); badly written code to refactor and a banking chatbot discussion (CO5); "Read some code, tell me what it does, and improve it." (CO7); product design plus light log-stream coding in Java or Python (CO8).
  4. Behavioral STAR questions (CO2, CO5, CO6, CO9).
- **Frontend loop versus the generic loop.** Generic software-engineer loops (CO30 to CO64) use a backend system design (a bank system, a credit card application system, a payment system) and a multi-level object-oriented coding exercise on CodeSignal. In 2026 a Capital One-badged commenter wrote "the system design and case interview will both be credit card directed." (CO36). A front-end-focused engineer in a 2025 full-stack loop met the backend design round and "found gaps in my back-end design knowledge despite studying" (CO16). A 2023 lead wrote "I guess the design problem might vary if you are applying for a front-end or data engineer role." (CO59). Whether the 2025 to 2026 Front-End Engineer loops still have the frontend design and frontend technical rounds: NOT FOUND.
- **Official text.** The only official description of a technology loop is written for AI and ML roles (CO-O1): a recruiter screen, an "algorithmic coding assessment" of "70 minutes, four questions", "Think LeetCode.", "the assessment is pass/fail", a resume review and "a 30-minute interview with the hiring manager", then Power Day, "four hour-long interviews covering technical coding, system design, behavioral interviews and a case study". It adds that "all four rounds carry equal weight in the final consensus". An official description of a frontend loop: NOT FOUND. An official rule on candidates using AI tools: NOT FOUND.
- **Hiring signal on 2026-10-23.** Workday lists seven frontend-titled postings: five Front-end Engineer 4 roles (McLean VA and New York NY; one adds Richmond VA; one is "Manager, IC") and two Front-end Engineer 5 roles (McLean VA; one is "Remote-eligible"). Engineer 4 asks for "At least 4 years of professional software engineering experience". Engineer 5 asks for "At least 6 years of professional experience in software engineering". All name "React, Angular, Vue, Svelte". One title adds "(JavaScript, TypeScript, Angular)". Capital One-badged users on Blind reported a freeze in April 2026: "we are no longer hiring PAs and below" (CO36) and "We have a hiring freeze with the potential layoff as the upper management focuses more on using AI." (CO46). How the new "Engineer 4" title maps to the older Principal Associate level: NOT FOUND.
- **What ranks for `capital one frontend interview questions`** (our WebSearch tool, 2026-10-23): Glassdoor review pages RVW68366224, RVW53357742, RVW92290077 and RVW95023086 on several country domains, and interviewpal's "capital one interview questions" template. No editorial frontend guide appears in the results.
- **Readiness.** Enough for a content-only guide, if the page dates its evidence honestly. The frontend-specific rounds rest on Taro rewrites of Glassdoor reviews from 2022 to 2024. The assessment, the case round and the behavioral round are confirmed by Blind, LeetCode and the official page through 2026.

## Loop by level and region

Official text. The general page (CO-O2) lists the steps before Power Day: "Recruiter Screen: A Capital One recruiter will have an initial phone conversation with you about the position, your resume and the enterprise.", "Virtual Test: Depending on the position, you may have to take an hour-long virtual exam that tests your knowledge of the position." and "Hiring Manager Pre-Screen: After you pass the recruiter screen and virtual testing, a recruiter will set up a 30-minute phone interview with the hiring manager." For Power Day it says "Depending on the position, the interviews may include" a job fit interview ("All candidates will complete one or two job fit interviews during the Power Day."), a behavioral interview and a case interview. It also says "Capital One requires that candidates use video for all virtual interviews." The AI and ML page (CO-O1) gives the technology version: a recruiter screen, a 70-minute assessment of four questions, a resume review, a 30-minute hiring manager interview, then four hour-long interviews in technical coding, system design, behavioral and a case. Interviews run on Zoom (CO-O3, CO-O5). Frontend-specific official text: NOT FOUND. Cities below are as Taro shows them, and Taro may change cities.

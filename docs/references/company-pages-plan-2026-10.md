# Company pages plan: sourced questions, one company per step, Waymo first

> Working plan approved on 2026-10-14. It is updated at the end of every step (status table at the bottom). Research behind it: `docs/references/interview-prep-market-research-2026-10.md` (what sells, what is missing, which companies are open and sourced) and `docs/references/ai-product-frontend-track-2026-10.md` (the AI track the Waymo page also feeds).

## Why

Company-specific interview content is the only product type in this market with verified revenue (Dataford, about $36.8K MRR). Competitors either wrote editorial guides for US big tech (GreatFrontEnd, 37 companies) or publish templated pages for any employer (Dataford, Prepfully, PracHub, techinterview.org). A page wins only as a worked guide whose prompts cite public interview reports. Four segments were checked; in the AI segment the strongest open and sourced company is Waymo (a May 2026 senior frontend loop reported on PracHub and read in full).

## Decisions recorded on 2026-10-14

- Step by step, one company per step, Waymo first.
- Every company question carries sources, rendered at the bottom of the question description; a compact line also appears on locked premium pages.
- New Waymo items: the coding question is free (indexable entry), the Angular debug item is premium.
- Company previews are rendered from one data-driven template fed by a content registry keyed by slug; the existing OpenAI, Google and Netflix branches stay untouched.
- Interview-report links get `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`; official company sources keep the existing rel.
- The editorial stance holds: "Editorial practice groupings, not verified official interview questions or endorsements" (`frontend/src/app/core/content/public-editorial-facts.ts:19-20`). The phrases "candidate reports" and "known questions" stay banned (`frontend/scripts/public-trust-regressions.test.mjs:186-196`); the same test bans `reviewedBy` and "internal review" in every app `.ts/.html` file. Wording to use: "public interview reports", "practice version modelled on a reported round".
- Trivia is out of scope: reference blocks are stripped on purpose and `seo-smoke.mjs:351-385` rejects `citation` and "Source check" text on trivia pages.

## Facts the plan rests on

- Questions link to a company only through `companies: string[]` on the entry. A company with no tagged question gets no prerender route (`frontend/scripts/generate-prerender-routes.mjs:209-255`), no sitemap entry (`frontend/scripts/seo-content-inventory.mjs:536-552`) and no directory card (`company-public-data.service.ts:31`, `counts.all > 0`); `audit-internal-link-equity.mjs:233-238` requires every sitemap preview to be linked from `/companies`. Cards count coding, trivia and system design, not debug.
- Custom previews are nested template branches in `company-preview.component.html` (OpenAI :12, Google :150-151, Netflix :341-342, generic :567). The Netflix content types (`netflix-preview-content.ts:1-45`), its "Sources and evidence limits" section (html :528-545) and `publishNetflixSeo` (ts :518-593) are the model for the new template.
- Hard-coded slug lists: `company-public-data.service.ts:18`, `seo-content-inventory.mjs:129, 303, 540`, `seo-date-parity.mjs:6-7`, `audit-internal-link-equity.mjs:13`, `company-index.component.html:42-55`, `frontend/src/llms.txt:76-86`, per-company blocks in `seo-smoke.mjs`.
- No `sources` field exists. Unknown fields pass `lint-questions.mjs` (563-623), loaders spread them (`question.service.ts:445-462`, `system-design.model.ts:188-240`), list items are whitelisted, `backend/services/gamification/question-catalog.js` ignores them. System design `meta.json` already has a lint-only `editorial.companyEvidence[]` that must mirror `companies` exactly (`lint-system-design-editorial-quality.mjs:547-566`).
- Render points: coding and debug share `CodingDetailComponent` (description panel `coding-detail.component.html:67-439`, locked view :1020); system design sections loop :340-518, `sd-related` :520, locked view :591. Locked views hide the description; `premiumPreview` is frozen to three keys (`premium-preview-content.test.mjs:54`).
- Pipeline: `cd frontend && npm run gen:data`, `npm run gen:prerender`, `node scripts/update-question-id-manifest.mjs`; sitemap and `seo-content-dates.ts` are gitignored and resolve from git history after commit. Coding assist sync fires on starter, tests or approach-code changes (`validate-coding-assist-sync.mjs:83-106`), not on tags. The interview registry pins the whole object of 60 coding questions by sha256 (`backend/content/interview/tools/interview-content-lib.mjs:264-275`); adding a field to a pinned entry breaks `generate:interview-content:check`. Adding a field to an existing free entry re-dates it in the sitemap.
- The Angular debug entry shape (`cdn/questions/angular/debug.json`, one item): `id, title, difficulty, importance, updatedAt, tags, description{text}, sdk{asset, openFile}, solutionAsset, solutionBlock{overview}, frameworkTests[]`; no `access` field. Sandbox assets under `cdn/sb/angular/{question,solution,debug}/` must be byte-identical to `frontend/src/assets/sb` (`format-framework-css.mjs --write`).

## Risks

- Re-dating: adding `sources` or `companies` to an existing free entry moves its lastmod and the aggregating hubs; tag and source each entry in one commit.
- Hash pins: whole-object sha256 on 60 coding entries; Step 3 avoids pinned ids; later tagging needs a registry re-pin with a version bump.
- Premium: `/companies/waymo/*` stays noindex (`vercel.json:39-58`); compact sources on locked pages are safe but never enter `premiumPreview`.
- SSR: `safe-html.pipe` returns raw HTML on the server, so sources render through attribute bindings only; the URL lint enforces `https://`.
- Mobile: narrow description pane; forced wrapping, no tables; check at 360px.
- Banned phrases: the Waymo module must avoid `reviewedBy`, "internal review", "candidate reports", "known questions", "How Waymo Evaluates".
- Spelling: "Waymo" may fail cspell in system design scope; add company names to `frontend/cspell/project-words.txt` if needed.
- Angular debug premium: if the debug kind does not honour `access`, the item ships free and this document records it.
